Products

What is ChatFi Wallet?

A mobile wallet for sending, receiving, swapping and earning on Solana.

What is ChatFi Pay?

Payment infrastructure for merchants: payment links, QR codes and Solana Pay, verified on-chain.

What is ChatFi Store?

A shop builder with themes, products, orders and analytics, with checkout powered by ChatFi Pay.

What is the redacted product?

We cannot say yet. It is being built and will plug into everything else.

Payments

Which currencies can I use?

Stablecoins such as USDC, SOL and Nigerian naira.

How long does a payment take?

Solana settles in seconds, and ChatFi Pay confirms the payment automatically once it lands.
