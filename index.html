How it connects

One loop, from wallet to checkout.

The products share one backend, so money, orders and identity move between them without glue code.

Hold

Customers keep funds in ChatFi Wallet and approve payments in a tap.

Pay

ChatFi Pay creates the payment, verifies it on-chain and confirms it to the merchant.

Sell

ChatFi Store turns each confirmed payment into an order, a receipt and analytics.

FAQ

What is ChatFi NetTech?

The parent company behind the ChatFi products. We build and run the shared backend that powers Wallet, Pay and Store.

Do the products work together?

Yes. They share one backend, so a payment made from Wallet can settle through Pay and become an order in Store.

Can I build on your backend?

Developer access is being opened up. Email us and we will get you started.
