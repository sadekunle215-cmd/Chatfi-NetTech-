About ChatFi NetTech.

The parent company behind the ChatFi products, building the backend that makes money, commerce and play work together.

Our story

Why we exist.

Money, shopping and play usually live in separate apps with separate accounts. We think they should share one foundation.

ChatFi NetTech is the parent company that builds and runs that foundation, so Wallet, Pay, Store and what comes next all work as one.

Values

What we believe.

Build the core once

One backend means every new product starts with payments, accounts and orders already solved.

Ship small, ship often

We release in small steps and fix what real merchants and customers run into.

Local roots, global reach

Based in Nigeria and built so anyone, anywhere, can pay and sell.
